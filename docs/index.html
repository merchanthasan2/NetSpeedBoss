What it does

Everything about your connection, nothing in your way.

Speed on your taskbar

Blends into the Windows taskbar, follows light and dark mode, steps aside for full-screen games and hides when the taskbar auto-hides.

Live speed graph

Watch the last 1 to 10 minutes of traffic. Hover anywhere on the graph for the exact speed at that moment, plus your peak and session totals.

The five busiest apps sit under the graph. Tick one to draw its own line, or open the Apps tab for every connection.

Floating meter & Gaming HUD

Metal-style transparent overlay that auto-shows in Windows GPU games (DX9–DX12, Vulkan, OpenGL) with RAG-colored FPS, VRAM, GPU temp/fan, multi-core CPU, and live game server ping.

Dual-WAN load balancing & Speedtest

Automatically sums dual internet links, shows live per-adapter speeds and gateways, and includes a built-in multi-stream Speedtest to measure combined bandwidth.

System, Hardware & ISP info

Live CPU, GPU & memory cards plus organized hardware, storage, BIOS, network, and public IP/ISP details with crisp manufacturer logos.

Light & Dark themes, private

Follows Windows Light/Dark mode automatically. Your data stays on your PC, with zero accounts, telemetry or ads. MIT-licensed by Quantum Step.

Want exact per-app speeds?

Windows only lets administrators read live per-app network traffic. Right-click the meter and choose Restart as administrator to see the speed of every app, including games, video calls and streaming. Without it, you still see which apps are connected and where to.

Questions

Good to know.

Is it really free?

Yes. NetSpeed Boss is published by Quantum Step under the MIT license — free and open source with no ads, upsells or accounts.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

That SmartScreen message appears for new `.exe` installers that are not yet code-signed. Click More info, then Run anyway. The full source code is on GitHub and every release is built automatically from it by GitHub Actions. Installing via Scoop, Winget, or Microsoft Store avoids the prompt.

Will it slow down my PC or my internet?

No. It reads the counters Windows already keeps, once a second, and uses a fraction of a percent of CPU. It never sends or downloads anything itself.

How do I move or customise the taskbar meter?

Drag it left or right along the taskbar. Right-click for quick options such as Bold taskbar text, or double-click and open Settings for font, size and colors.

Where is my data stored? (Privacy Policy)

Only on your PC — NetSpeed Boss collects zero personal data, telemetry, or analytics. Usage logs (daily, hourly and per-app CSV files that open in Excel) live in Documents\NetSpeed Boss. Settings live in %APPDATA%\NetSpeedBoss.

How do I update or uninstall?

Update with scoop update netspeedboss, winget upgrade Net.NetSpeedBoss, choco upgrade netspeedboss, or by running a newer installer. Uninstall from Settings → Apps or your package manager.
